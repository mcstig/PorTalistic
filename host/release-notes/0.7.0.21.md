## What's new in 0.7.0.21

**Seven more emulators in Desktop Mode**
- Desktop Mode now also comes with Dolphin (GameCube, Wii), Azahar (3DS), PPSSPP (PSP), xemu (the original Xbox), melonDS (DS), Flycast (Dreamcast) and MAME (arcade), installed in the background with the others and pinned to the taskbar after them.
- Dolphin, Azahar, PPSSPP, xemu and Flycast start on Vulkan, so they draw on the Mac's GPU (a setting you change later is kept).
- All eleven emulators take about 5 GB on the Linux system's disk on a new install; they're skipped if there isn't room.

**Full screen**
- Restart Steam (and the Steam menu's other jobs) no longer switches you out of full screen to show its progress: the Activity window opens over Portalistic's window, Restart Steam's closes by itself when Steam is back, and Portalistic comes back to the front.
