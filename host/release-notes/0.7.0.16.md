## What's new in 0.7.0.16

**Copy and paste between the Mac and Steam**
- Text copied on the Mac pastes in the VM, and text copied in the VM lands on the Mac's clipboard. In the window ⌘C, ⌘V and ⌘X work as on the Mac (⌘⇧C and ⌘⇧V in a terminal). Text only, up to 1 MB.
- The pointer takes the VM's shapes: resize arrows at a window's edges, the I-beam over text.

**Full screen**
- Full screen hides the Mac's menu bar, also when System Settings keeps it shown ("Automatically hide and show the menu bar: Never"). It comes back, with the title bar and its full-screen button, when the pointer reaches the top of the screen.
- With a second display above the Mac's, passing the pointer up to it no longer flashes the menu bar.

**Desktop Mode**
- **The whole Mac is there**: your home folder (now writable) and every drive of the Mac, straight, not over the network. Dolphin lists each drive by its own name.
- **Emulators come with it**, pinned to the taskbar: Ryujinx (Nintendo Switch, the ARM build, on the Mac's GPU), RPCS3 (PlayStation 3), Play! (PlayStation 2) and RetroArch (older consoles; its cores come from Main Menu > Online Updater). They install in the background after Desktop Mode, about 4.5 GB; set Play! to Vulkan in its settings.
- **Flatpak apps from Discover use the Mac's GPU** (in Vulkan).
- The Steam menu shows only the switch to the other mode, and switching to Game Mode asks first (the desktop's apps close).
- The "Switching to Desktop Mode" window closes as soon as the desktop is up (it waited about 15 s for a network scan).

**Steam and games**
- **Decky Loader is built in**: Steam plugins from the Quick Access menu.
- Steam's interface always draws on the Mac's GPU, never on the processor.
- The first-start banner about installing a game's Windows components (Visual C++, DirectX…) shows only the first time, not at every start.
- Devil May Cry 5 and other RE Engine games that stop in DirectX 12 with an error box are switched to DirectX 11 and started again, and a notice says so.
- Shut Down the VM… closes Steam properly again (since Oct 28 it was skipped).
- Games with many graphics allocations no longer lose the GPU (more open files for the VM).
- A Mac folder that stops answering (a macOS privacy question, for one) no longer freezes every command Portalistic sends the VM.

**GOG**
- A game not installed shows what installing it takes (the download and the room on disk), and the free-space check before an install works again.

**New installs**
- The VM's disk is 25 GB from the start.

**Known issue**
- Counter-Strike 2 runs its Windows version again. It can still freeze on team selection or the first spawn; ⌘Q, then Quit, ends it.
