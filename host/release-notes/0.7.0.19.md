## What's new in 0.7.0.19

**Desktop Mode's taskbar**
- On a new desktop, adding the emulators to the taskbar replaced its own icons: System Settings, Discover, Dolphin and Firefox went missing. They're kept now, with the emulators after them, and a taskbar that lost them gets them back at the next switch to Desktop Mode.
- Emulators installed while the desktop was open could show as blank icons (a click opened an "Open with" list). Fixed, and Ryujinx shows its own icon on a new system too.

**Window shortcuts in Desktop Mode**
- Alt+Tab (Option+Tab on a Mac keyboard) switches windows, Alt+Shift+Tab goes back, and Alt+F4 closes a window. They were all switched off. A shortcut you set yourself is left as it is.
