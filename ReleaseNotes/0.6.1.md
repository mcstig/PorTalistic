## What's new in 0.6.1

- **Updates arrive by themselves.** PorTalistic checks when it opens and asks before installing anything. Choose Later and it waits.
- **Wine builds download as soon as a game needs them**, with progress at the bottom of the sidebar — no more waiting until you press Play.
- **Wine 11.16 (DXMT) is now the first choice**, for Direct3D 11 games and for 32-bit games such as BioShock Remastered.
- Fixed a game failing to start when it was launched while its Wine build was still downloading.
